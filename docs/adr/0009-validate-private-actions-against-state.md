# Keep private menus valid until their relevant state changes

Private bot menus must remain usable after elapsed time and restart. Use reusable navigation and fresh-interaction entry points, while mutation confirmations execute once under current item, operation, or setting evidence guards and record their local effect or deferred work transactionally. This gives up arbitrary timer-based expiry in favor of explicit state validation and durable receipts; legacy confirmations without sufficient evidence require a fresh review.
