# Prepare posts in the queue channel

Design decisions settled on 2026-10-15; implementation awaits final confirmation of the shared design. See the [implementation outline](queue-preparation-implementation-plan.md) and [hidden-sender nickname design](hidden-sender-nicknames.md).

The [2026-10-17 reliability decisions](telegram-reliability.md) supersede earlier admin-edit, shared-notification, and scheduled-retry rules: any admin edit freezes the whole post, safe scheduled retries have a fixed five-minute window, and recurring flood waits share one incident until stable recovery.

## Agreed behavior

- Apply publication rules promptly after a post is added to the queue. Replace user forwards with copies carrying `by <nickname>` or the sender-name fallback. Posts from repostable channels retain forwarding attribution. Other origin/content policies also apply during preparation.
- The prepared post visible in the queue is the version to deliver to the destination. Publication must not append the byline again or introduce forwarding attribution from the queue channel.
- Nickname changes and removals automatically update matching waiting posts' visible bylines. Those items cannot publish until the update is complete. An already-started publication keeps its captured version.
- Changes to the repostable-channel list apply only to later enqueues. Capture a new item's forwarding mode when it is enqueued, and retain it even if preparation is delayed or retried. Establish the mode for legacy waiting items from the current rules at rollout. Applying a changed channel rule to an existing item requires removing and enqueueing the post again. Original forward attribution cannot reliably be reconstructed from a prepared copy.
- Prepare existing waiting items at rollout while retaining item numbers, publishing order, and initial enqueue time.
- Preserve original messages until a complete prepared replacement is confirmed and recorded. Incomplete or uncertain preparation stays unpublishable; retain originals and resolve uncertainty before another attempt. Cleanup failures after adoption are tracked separately.
- Discover direct queue-channel deletions when normal preparation, refresh, renewal, or publication attempts copy or forward the messages. Reflect confirmed deletions in the active database queue at that point; immediate detection or a periodic check is not required. Keep the existing Bot API integration without a second Telegram connection or MTProto session. Network/access failures and ambiguous transfer results are not proof of deletion.
- Treat an admin's partial deletion as an intentional edit: remove the deleted members from the item's current representation and publish the remaining messages as left by the admin. Do not restore removed content or credits, mark the post incomplete, or raise an issue solely because of that deletion. Remove the item from the active queue when no current messages remain. Deletion of obsolete messages from earlier generations does not change the current post. Keep the existing terminal-history and audit-retention behavior; active queue removal does not require physically deleting historical records.
- Keep the publishing schedule and select the earliest eligible post, skipping items with issues or pending preparation/refresh. If a candidate fails before sending anything during a scheduled slot, try later candidates until one publication succeeds or no candidate remains. A preparation problem outside a slot does not trigger an unscheduled publication. Manual publish-next follows the same eligibility rule.
- Notify the admin privately when any item develops a new issue, with its number, current queue link, reason, and available resolution actions. Notify again for material changes or recurrence after resolution, not every minute for unchanged conditions. Failed notification delivery is retried independently and does not stop other publications.
- Admin edits take precedence over automatic attribution. Preserve edited content, media, captions, and formatting. Nickname changes update only an untouched bot-generated byline; an admin's rewritten or deleted byline, including deletion of its containing message, must not be restored automatically.
- If a publication might already have reached the destination, consume the current slot, notify the admin, and retain that item's uncertainty for explicit resolution without automatic resending. Other ready posts can publish at subsequent scheduled slots.
- A global Telegram outage, flood wait, or destination-permission failure stops the current publishing run and raises a shared notification. Respect retry/cooldown rules and keep other posts ready instead of marking every candidate faulty.
- Corrective admin edits automatically revalidate failed preparation. Prepare valid items and return them to normal scheduling without requiring Retry or triggering an unscheduled publication.
- A repaired item retains its original position among remaining posts. Moving it to the end remains an explicit admin action.
- Preview fidelity covers content, formatting, media, member order, album grouping, and forwarding attribution. Telegram-generated timestamps, view counts, reactions, and interactive state such as poll votes follow Telegram's transfer behavior.
- Recover untracked messages as shown by default, with an optional action to apply preparation rules. Restore known metadata where available; otherwise preserve the messages without inferring their original author or adding another byline. Automatic nickname refresh is unavailable without original sender metadata or an explicitly assigned sender.
- Require review when a shorter album-transfer result does not establish which messages were omitted or why. Preserve originals and recorded outputs, notify the admin, and do not automatically repeat the send. Confirmed intentional deletions remain normal edits. If the destination may already contain output, that attempt consumes the publishing slot.

## Constraints found in the current implementation

- Albums arrive as separate updates and require collection before preparation. Current album buffering uses a two-second quiet period and a ten-second maximum.
- Some content uses a separate byline message, so one prepared post can contain both a content message and a credit outside a Telegram album. Preparation, delivery, renewal, deletion, and recovery must handle the complete set.
- Preserve original semantic origin independently of the prepared messages and their delivery mode. A copied user post loses its Telegram forward origin but still needs the original sender metadata for nickname refresh.
- Existing renewal forwards most semantic origins. It must instead preserve the prepared form and avoid introducing queue-channel attribution.
- Preparation and nickname refresh need durable operations, readiness checks, and suppression of bot-generated messages/edits, including responses arriving after updates or across restarts.
- Telegram's [Bot API Update schema](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#update) has no ordinary channel-message deletion update. Missing sources are discovered through normal copy/forward results; database state can therefore lag behind direct deletions until a source is next used. Bulk-transfer responses must be handled carefully because they can skip sources without identifying which source was skipped.
- Existing publication selects the earliest nonterminal item even if it has an issue, and the scheduler tries it once per slot. Continuing with available posts requires eligibility-based selection and defined handling for failures during an attempt.

## Verification implications

- Preserve admin edits and deliberate member/byline removal during renewal as well as publication and nickname refresh; do not reconstruct from the initial submission.
- Wait for album collection before preparation and include separate credits in the same logical item for publication, renewal, and deletion.
- Do not map shortened bulk responses onto the first N original snapshots. Deleted first/middle/byline-bearing members can invalidate that mapping.
- Preserve the captured publication version after destination transfer begins; edits acknowledged before the claim apply, while later queue edits cannot retroactively change that publication.
- Confirmed missing messages are removed from active membership. Permission failures, unsupported content, network ambiguity, and incomplete responses must remain distinct from proven deletion.
